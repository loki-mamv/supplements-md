Quality Testing & Verification

✓ ConsumerLab

Independent testing of supplements for purity, potency, and label accuracy. Subscription required for full reports.

consumerlab.com

✓ USP Verified Products

Search for supplements that meet USP quality standards. USP verification is a trusted quality mark.

usp.org

✓ NSF Certified for Sport

Supplements tested for banned substances. Essential for athletes subject to drug testing.

nsfsport.com
